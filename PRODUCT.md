# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Experiment owners, business analysts, troubleshooting engineers, and platform administrators use this internal tool while reviewing active experiments, seed usage, rollout changes, imports, and conflict risk across teams.

## Product Purpose

The platform centralizes experiment records from different business systems so teams can quickly understand what is running, how traffic changed over time, which parent-child relationships exist, and whether a seed or experiment may cause conflicts. Success means faster cross-team troubleshooting, clearer ownership, and fewer hidden experiment collisions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboards, decorative cards, exposed admin controls for ordinary users, demo-only explanatory copy, oversized filters, and unfamiliar homemade controls. The interface should feel close to practical enterprise products such as Arco Pro, Semi Design, or Ant Design Pro, with denser information and quiet interaction states.

## Design Principles

1. Keep primary workflows visible: investigation, seed evaluation, validation, batch import, parent-child experiments, and rollout history.
2. Separate ordinary user and administrator capabilities without making role switching a distracting top-level control.
3. Prefer compact, familiar admin patterns over decorative layouts.
4. Use structured records, statuses, and history trails so issues can be reconstructed later.
5. Treat hints and help as progressive disclosure, not inline instruction blocks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Chinese-capable system font stacks, clear focus states, sufficient contrast for table and form text, icon plus text for navigation, and reduced visual noise for long daily sessions.
